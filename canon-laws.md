# Canon Laws

1. Hospitality assets are operating businesses.
2. Capital strategy and hospitality execution must be connected.
3. Experience must be underwritten, not added as decoration.
4. Operations create proof, and proof creates trust.
5. Scale depends on standards, not founder heroics.
6. Build the system. Prove the economics. Scale selectively.
7. One HoldCo. Two Reinforcing Engines.
8. Fragmented handoffs dilute value; integrated execution compounds it.
9. Financial and tax outcomes are coordinated with licensed professionals; no guarantees are made.

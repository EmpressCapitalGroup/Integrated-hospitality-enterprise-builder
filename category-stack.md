# Category Stack

**Owned Category:** Integrated Hospitality Enterprise Builder  
**Category Claim:** Hospitality assets are operating businesses, not merely passive real estate. Value is created through strategic integration, not isolated specialization.  
**The Reframe:** The market treats hospitality investment as a series of disconnected handoffs (capital → acquisition → property management). This category treats it as one operating system where capital strategy and hospitality execution must be connected to compound value.  
**Problem:** Fragmented hospitality investment and ownership functions operating without shared strategy or accountability.  
**Misbelief:** Hospitality assets are primarily passive real estate optimized by disconnected specialists.  
**New Perspective:** Capital strategy and hospitality execution must be connected. Scale depends on standards, not founder heroics.  
**Differentiation:** Not limited to a fund manager, property manager, designer, operator, or consultant. Connects investment strategy and operating execution across the enterprise.

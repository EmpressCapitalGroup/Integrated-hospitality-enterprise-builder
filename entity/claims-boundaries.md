# Claims & Boundaries

**Includes:** Strategic integration, enterprise architecture, connected underwriting, experiential value creation, launch execution, revenue management, hospitality operations, asset oversight, reporting, and system-driven scale.

**Excludes:** Fragmented hospitality execution, disconnected advisory or vendor handoffs, property management treated as the entire value-creation model, guaranteed tax or financial promises, unverified performance claims, and founder-dependent scale without standards or accountability.

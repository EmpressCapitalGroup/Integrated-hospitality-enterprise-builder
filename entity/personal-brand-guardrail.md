# Personal-Brand Guardrail

“Integrated Hospitality Enterprise Builder” is George Salas’s personal authority category, not merely a description of Empress Hospitality Group.  
Empress Hospitality Group is the enterprise expression and extension of George’s category. The Canon reflects George’s personal patterns of strategic integration, enterprise building, leadership, decision-making, capital allocation, and operating philosophy.  
The personal brand leads, while Empress serves as the operating proof, enterprise platform, and commercial expression of that authority. Do not collapse George’s personal authority into a company profile. Do not broaden the personal brand beyond hospitality that the authority territory becomes unclear.

# Framework: One HoldCo. Two Reinforcing Engines.

**Status:** Approved Structural Architecture

The enterprise is organized under one holding company with two reinforcing engines:
1. **Capital & Asset Management** — Deployment, underwriting, acquisition, financing, reporting, and oversight.
2. **Hospitality Operations & Value Creation** — Design, launch, revenue management, guest experience, property condition, and operational execution.

**Principle:** The two engines operate in continuous feedback. Capital decisions are informed by operational data; operational execution is funded and scaled by capital strategy.

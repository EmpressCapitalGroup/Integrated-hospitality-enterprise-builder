# Framework: Strategic Integration

**Status:** Approved Core Capability; Formal Framework Wording Working

The systematic connection of capital deployment, asset acquisition, operational execution, and experiential design into a single accountability loop. Replaces the fragmented "handoff" model with a unified enterprise architecture.

**Core Function:** Eliminates disconnected capital, design, and operations silos by routing all execution through one integrated decision framework.
**Output:** Connected underwriting, aligned launch execution, and continuous operational feedback loops.

# Governance & Update Protocol

This repository is governed by strict content controls to maintain canonical integrity.

- **Source of Truth:** `George_Salas_Authority_Canon_v2_FINAL.md`
- **Modification Rule:** No file may be altered without founder approval. Working or Draft concepts do not convert to Approved by default.

- **Versioning:** All changes require a new version tag and changelog entry.

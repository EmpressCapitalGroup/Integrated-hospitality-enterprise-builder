# Proof Hierarchy

Proof is classified by verification level and operational status.

1. **Completed Operating Proof / Founder-Verified Operating Fact** — Assets acquired, repositioned, launched, and currently generating performance data.
2. **Current Transaction Development and In-Progress Strategy Evidence** — Active acquisitions, conversion strategies, or partnership structuring underway.
3. **Approved Documented Methodology** — Repeatedly applied systems with founder-verified transaction history.
4. **Documented Internal Architecture** — Systems and controls in place to transition from founder-led to system-led execution.

**Rule:** Proof must be documented, not promised. No unverified financial attribution, institutional scale, or M&A claims are published.

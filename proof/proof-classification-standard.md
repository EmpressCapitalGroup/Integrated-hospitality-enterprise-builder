# Proof Classification Standard

All proof in this repository is categorized by verification level:

- **Completed Operating Proof / Founder-Verified Operating Fact** — Live assets with documented performance.
- **Current Transaction Development and In-Progress Strategy Evidence** — Active deals with documented strategy and execution milestones.
- **Approved Documented Methodology** — Systems applied across multiple transactions with founder verification.
- **Documented Internal Architecture** - Internal systems, controls, and reporting standards in place.

**Rule:** Proof is never presented as guaranteed returns, institutional scale, or completed M&A unless explicitly verified and documented. Unverified financial attribution is excluded from public claims.

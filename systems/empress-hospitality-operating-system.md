# System: Empress Hospitality Operating System

**Status:** Approved Documented Methodology

The integrated hospitality methodology organized around nine operating standards. It connects strategy, underwriting, design, launch, revenue management, guest experience, property condition, reporting, and risk and compliance across the two reinforcing engines.

**Components:**
- Strategic Alignment
- Connected Underwriting
- Experiential Design Integration
- Launch Execution
- Revenue & Pricing Management
- Guest Experience Standards
- Property Condition & Maintenance
- Performance Reporting
- Risk & Compliance Protocols

**Purpose:** Provides a repeatable, standards-driven operating layer that ensures consistency, accountability, and compounding proof across all assets.
